What's changing and what you need to do

Tucson Orthopaedic Institute's email, Teams, OneDrive, and SharePoint are moving to a new Microsoft 365 environment managed by Orthopedic Care Partners (OCP).

Your email address and password do not change. However, each app on each device you use needs to be reconnected to the new environment. Select your device below and follow the steps for each app.

📧 Outlook / Email 💬 Microsoft Teams ☁️ OneDrive 📁 SharePoint
Select your device

iPhone — iOS

Sign out and back in for each app below

  1. Open the Outlook app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your profile photo or initials in the top-left corner.
  3. Tap the gear icon (Settings) at the bottom of the sidebar.
  4. Tap your @tucsonortho.com email account near the top.
  5. Scroll down and tap Delete Account, then confirm.
  6. Return to the Accounts list and tap Add Email Account.
  7. Enter your email address and follow the prompts to sign in.
💡 If asked, choose Work or school account when prompted for account type.
  1. Open the Teams app.
  2. Tap your profile photo in the top-left corner.
  3. Scroll down and tap Sign out, then confirm.
  4. On the sign-in screen, tap Sign in and enter your email address.
  5. Enter your password and complete any multi-factor authentication (MFA) prompts.
  1. Open the OneDrive app.
  2. Tap your profile photo or initials (top left).
  3. Tap the gear icon to open Settings.
  4. Tap your account name.
  5. Tap Sign Out.
  6. Tap Sign in and enter your email address and password.

Mac — macOS

Sign out and back in for each app below

  1. Open Outlook.
  2. In the menu bar, click Tools → Accounts…
  3. In the left panel, click your @tucsonortho.com account.
  4. Click the minus (−) button at the bottom of the account list.
  5. Click Delete to confirm.
  6. Click the plus (+) button and choose New Account…
  7. Enter your email address and click Continue, then follow the sign-in prompts.
💡 Outlook may need a restart after adding the new account before your calendar and contacts fully sync.
  1. Open Teams.
  2. Click your profile picture in the top-right corner.

  1. Click the OneDrive cloud icon in the menu bar (top-right area).

  3. Click the Account tab.
  4. Click Unlink this Mac.
  5. In the OneDrive setup window that appears, enter your email address and follow prompts to sign in.

Classic Outlook (older interface)

New Outlook (modern interface)

  2. Click Accounts → Email accounts in the left panel.
  3. Click your @tucsonortho.com account, then click Remove account and confirm.
  4. Click Add account, enter your email address, and follow the sign-in steps.

Web Browser

Works on any device — Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox

  1. Go to office.com (or any Microsoft 365 web app like Outlook or Teams).
  2. Click your profile photo or initials in the top-right corner.
  3. Click Sign out.
  4. On the sign-in page, click Use a different account (or Sign in with a different account).
  5. Enter your email address and click Next.
  6. Enter your password and complete any MFA prompts to finish signing in.
💡 Signing out at office.com signs you out of Outlook, Teams, OneDrive, and SharePoint all at once in the browser.
  1. Open a Private / Incognito window (Ctrl+Shift+N on Windows/Chrome, Cmd+Shift+N on Mac/Chrome, Ctrl+Shift+P Firefox/Edge).
  2. Go to office.com in the private window.
  3. Sign in with your account — this bypasses any cached session.

If the private window works but the regular window still shows the old account, you can clear Microsoft-related cookies:

  1. In your browser settings, go to Privacy & Security → Clear browsing data.
  2. Select Cookies and site data for All time.
  3. Click Clear data, then sign back in at office.com with your account.
  1. In Edge, click your profile picture in the top-right of the browser window.
  2. If your old @tucsonortho.com account is listed, click Sign out next to it.
  3. Click Sign in and add your account.
  4. Choose to sync your browser data with the new account if prompted.
